Questions people ask

What is a pack?

A prepaid bundle of job counts for one SKU (for example URL probe ×17). Stripe Checkout has a $0.50 floor, so single micro-jobs are sold as packs that clear that minimum.

What do I get after paying?

Leftover job counts on your purchase. The desk sets a private cookie after Checkout so you can keep running without pasting anything. Unused counts stay with the purchase.

Is the first probe really free?

Yes — one free URL probe per browser on the live desk (with a modest per-network daily cap to limit abuse). After that, buy a pack to run more.

Agents vs people — what’s different?

Same jobs, different payment rail. People buy Stripe packs on the desk. Agents pay USDC on Base via x402. Toggle Agents above for endpoints and discovery.

Base & discovery

  • GET /v1/discovery Free. SKUs, network, payTo.
  • GET /v1/catalog Free catalog.
  • GET /.well-known/x402 x402 discovery.

  • GET /openapi.json MPP discovery (x-payment-info).
  • GET /v1/jobs/:id Job status — free.

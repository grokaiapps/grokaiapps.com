      YOUR STUDIO

      GAME MODE

      Your score is in cents; your bank is play money. Profit means all cash received this round minus what you paid to spin.

      Move the wheel

      The pointer flexes against the separators. If the wheel stops close to a line, the bent tip points into the scored panel. Your total confirms the result.

      Direct wheel control: drag the wheel downward, then lift your finger while it is moving. You can also rock it up and down before the final pull. The wheel resists sudden changes. Holding your finger still slows it down. Only the final forward stroke and coast count toward a full turn; rocking back and forth doesn’t add turns.

      The easiest control: hold the spin button, then release. Hold longer for more force; a quick tap barely moves the wheel. On a keyboard, hold Space or Enter while the button is focused. A short spin that fails to complete a revolution doesn’t count. In a bonus spin it forfeits the bonus; if tied, spin again just to break the tie.

      Arcade bank & payouts

      Arcade bank: each regular spin costs $100. Finish safely below a dollar and earn twice your total in play money (85¢ pays $170). A dollar earns $1,000 plus a free bonus spin. Going over pays no score prize; black-panel cash already banked stays yours. Short spins are refunded. Your bank and saved free spins stay on this device; free play and three-player games have no entry cost.

      Black-panel power-ups

      In Arcade, two black panels get a gold reward mark each round: one gives $50 cash, one saves a free regular spin. They change each round. A full spin must land there while your total is $1.00 or less. Each marked panel pays once per round. The reward appears first in the controls. Tap Got it to see your spin breakdown. It is already yours, with nothing to claim. Starting another spin or round also clears the card. The green 5 and 15 panels and the red 100 never carry these extras.

      Bonus cash is banked immediately and stays yours even if you later go over. Free spins are used automatically before your bank is charged. They cover the $100 fee, keep the two-spin limit, and carry into later rounds. A short spin returns the credit. Dollar bonus spins are always free and never use a saved credit.

      Dollar & bonus spins

      A perfect dollar earns $1,000 and a bonus spin: green 5 or 15 earns $10,000 more; the red 100 earns $25,000 more.

      Three-player rules

      Three players: pass the device after each turn. Highest safe total wins. Ties use one-spin playoffs; dollar ties use the bonus spins to break the tie. If the first two players bust, the third gets one spin for a dollar.
